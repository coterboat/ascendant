# The Lacquer Reed, round 2

## What changed

The people, Low Note, the Hollowing, and the Wright-stance are the same. This round names why the two rulers rule, gives Chimewater three build tiers, and says what the Reed does to land it holds.

## The two rulers

**Yssa of the Three**, mortal line, second rank. She Hollowed with two siblings; they stopped Half-poured and she went through. She rules because the Solid and the Half-poured still vote, and she is the hollowed one they will sit beside. **Quiet Audience:** her province earns +2/3/4/5 gold if no enemy is in it or next to it. Heir in two turns at half power.

Pick Yssa for a quiet purse. Park her at Chimewater, spend power on mandates, keep the woods empty so the gold pays. Empty her pool and she dies in the second rank. A mid-game sacrifice still costs +1.

**The Pale Note**, god, second rank. The marsh was already an instrument; this is the missing voice that answered. It rules because a chord wants a conductor, and it bristles at being called the Wright. **Fearful Pitch:** enemies here start at −1 morale, or −2 if it has kept 6 or more power. Returns next turn at −3 power for good.

Pick the Pale Note to fight in the wood. Keep power in the god, lead into a forest you already hold, and let Low Note and the morale drop stack. Each death makes the god smaller.

At 96×128: Yssa is a teal living face in a bone-white bell, three-hole reed in hand. The Pale Note is a pale violet-lit face in a resin-flooded bell, holding a glowing fork.

## The commander

The **Chormeister** conducts from behind the wall, gold baton the held item. **True Pitch:** hit chance on its side cannot fall below 50%. Answer to a Kilnborn wall. One per army, plus the usual +2 morale while it stands.

## The fortress and capital

**Chimewater** is a fan of glossy teal pipes on lashed barges around a drained dark-water court, the **Sounding Bowl**. Fisher-folk hollowed the first pipes from living reed and lacquered them. Muster: the comb files through the Bowl and out the gate-pipes.

Tiers: (1) barges and a drained bowl; (2) a ring of short teal pipes as the wall, two taller pipes as the gate, the Bowl as courtyard, fort +2 defense; (3) the keep-pipe with a violet glow in its mouth, extra barge-platforms. Repeatable kit: barge deck, short wall-pipe with a bone-white bell cap, taller gate-pipe, keep-pipe, the Bowl.

## The mark on the land

They hollow land they hold because the marsh was already a chord missing voices. Newly taken: teal lacquer on trunks, violet at the water. Held a while: trees stand as pipes with bone-white caps, cottage roofs become bells, the stream stills. Heartland: the forest is a comb; the road and cottages are still there, played.

When they lose it, the lacquer cracks and the violet dries. The hollow trunks stay. A new owner inherits a whistling wood until someone fills the holes.

Cheap 3D: a teal ground tint, pipe-reed props on the forest edge, a violet lip on water, a bell-roof swap on cottages. Low quality keeps the tint only. Violet motes on high.

Rule seed, design owns the number: a forest held two turns or more keeps Low Note for one turn after they lose it. The forecast shows the linger.

## Rivalry and ally

**Sharpest rivalry:** the Thornmane Clans. Both want the same wood. If the Reed already holds it, Low Note pays and Wild Ground can pay on the Clan attack; the report should name both.

**Unlikeliest ally:** the Candlewake. The Resolved Chord ends the day on a rest; the Wake wants death finished so there is somewhere to lie down.
