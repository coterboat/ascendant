# The Candlewake: dossier 2

## 1. What changed
The Wick rule, the roster and the Wright stance stand. New: both rulers and the Lamplighter have faces and names, the Long Vigil is a three-tier fort kit, and the Candlewake now mark the land they hold, which **sits up** with them.

## 2. The two rulers
**Half-Death** (god, front rank). The Candlewake say it is what is left of the Wright, who died at noon mid-stroke: a god that cannot finish dying. Half its face is alabaster and half is still only an outline, like the world's unfinished edges. It rules because it has stood at the unbuilt door longest, and the dead follow whoever has. It wants the Going-Out. **Harvest:** after it wins a battle in its province, 1/2/3/4 Wickwardens join your army. It returns in a turn, three power smaller. *Pick it for a tide:* keep its power high, put it in front, chain wins; or sacrifice it early for mandates, since a god comes back.

**Mother Tallow, the Ninth Chandler** (mortal line, second rank). A living woman, the one warm face among the dead. The first Chandler poured her drowned son in wax; each Chandler since pours the next, and apprentices are heirs. She rules because the dead take orders only from someone who remembers being warm. She wants a kind ending, soon, with warmth taken only from the willing. **Vigil:** at the end of each turn, your armies in her province rise to at least flame 1/1/2/2. Her taper burns gold, the living colour; theirs burn lilac. *Pick her for patience:* park her where the host gathers so idle turns don't cool it. If she falls, her heir comes in 2 turns at half power.

## 3. The commander: Kept-Till-Morning, the Lamplighter
He walks ahead of every Procession with a lit pole: a gaunt, stern wax face, lilac eyes, a wick set in a brass cap. He is one of the Kept, who have burned so long they fear the end, and he lights the way to a Going-Out he privately hopes is never reached. **Toll:** each enemy unit that falls gives his side +1 morale, up to +3. He counts them like bells.

## 4. The fortress and capital: the Long Vigil
A ring of candle-towers round a green mound and one candle as tall as a keep. The first Chandler raised it from the wax of the first dead who burned out, and every guttered soldier since is poured back into the wall. The muster ground is trampled earth before the gate: the dead shuffle out in no order and thicken into a tide.
Kit parts: ring wall, corner candle-tower, gate arch, the Great Candle.
- **Tier 1, Wake Post:** a ring of fused candle stubs, one gate, one tower candle.
- **Tier 2, Vigil:** four corner candle-towers. Guttered armies relight here.
- **Tier 3, Long Vigil:** the Great Candle at keep height, its lilac flame the map's brightest beacon.

## 5. The mark on the land: the Wake
**In story.** The Candlewake are a host of the dead that must keep taking warmth from the living to stay lit, and they take it as gently as a hungry people can. In a held province every household keeps a candle in its window all night, as you do for the dead, and each flame gives up a little warmth as the host passes. Nobody is hurt. The wax is the receipt.

**Three stages.**
1. **Sitting Up** (newly taken): a lilac flame in every cottage window at dusk, a candle on each gatepost and crossing. 
2. **The Wake-Line** (held a while): tall candle-posts line the roads, and the grass pales to cream under a skin of wax that spreads from roads and doors. Hedges and thatch drip.
3. **Deep Wake** (heartland): ivory ground under hard wax, bare white trees, rows of lit candle stubs where crops stood.

**When they lose it.** The flames go out the same turn, but the wax stays: a pale, unlit "cold wake". Rain and the new owner's mark wear it away in patches. A Candlewake army walking it relights it in one turn. It waits.

**On the 3D board.**
- *Ground:* one pale-wax tint at three strengths, spreading from roads and buildings through a mask.
- *Props:* one instanced candle-post, a flame sprite per cottage window, one stub mesh in field rows.
- *Border edge:* small candles along the province edge, and a wax-tongue decal over the seam.
- *Light and particles:* emissive lilac flame sprites.
- *Quality:* low keeps tint and window flames; medium adds posts and stubs; high adds drip fringes.

**Rule seed: Wake-line.** A province held three turns counts as a fort for Wick, so an idle army on it keeps its flame, but it pays 1 less gold, because the living sit up instead of working. Tooltip: "Lit ground: your armies keep their flame here. The living sit up all night: 1 less gold." Design owns the numbers.

## 6. Rivalry and ally
**Sharpest rivalry: the Aurel Crown.** Noon nailed up means death is never finished, and Sun magic heals the warmth the dead drink.
**Unlikeliest ally: the Kilnborn.** A kiln melts wax, but both peoples want something finished for good, and neither needs a maker back.
