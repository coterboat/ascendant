# The Kilnborn: dossier 2

## 1. What changed
The rule (Dug In), the roster and the Potter stance stand. New: the rulers, commander and fortress have faces and names, the Great Kiln is a three-tier fort kit, and the Kilnborn now **fire the land they hold**.

## 2. The two rulers
**The Kiln-Mothers** (undying, second rank). Hest Turquoise, Calda Celadon and Brin Oxblood have fired every Kilnborn child for three hundred years, and speak in turn. They rule because nobody is a person here until they have walked into the kiln. They want the Last Firing, kindly: a fired thing cannot be hurt by being unmade. **Refire** heals one chosen regular unit 4/8/12/16 hp a turn in their province; they re-form in four turns at full power. *Pick them for a patient game:* forts, Dug In, one veteran who never dies, the High Fire fork. They stand behind the line, so the units win it.

**The Unfired** (god, front rank). The first jar that stood up: soft wet clay, a house tall, the one Kilnborn who refused every kiln. It rules because every Kilnborn descends from it, and it alone grieves what the kiln costs. It wants no child fired before they are sure, and is not sure it is right. **First Shape** gives units recruited in its province +1/2/3/4 maximum hp. When it falls it is back in a turn, three power smaller, with a stranger's thumbprint in it. *Pick it for a front-line game:* a walking barracks, cheap to sacrifice for a mandate, and the road to the Slip fork.

## 3. The commander: Kess Saltglaze, the Kiln-Master
A broad man who fired his shape at sixteen, as a soldier, and has never said he regrets it. He wears slab-tile armour, and the army forms on his standard, a kiln paddle carrying a clay disc kept glowing hot. After every battle he walks the line and re-staples the survivors' cracks in copper: **Mend**, 4 hp each. He is gentle only with broken things.

## 4. The fortress and capital: the Great Kiln
A working kiln the size of a hill, and the only place a Kilnborn can be fired. The first Kiln-Mothers built it of brick, and the brick is every self a child decided not to be: each soft first shape is fired and laid in the wall. The muster ground is a pale tile floor before the gate. Each tile is one place in the ranks, so the army stands in a grid, then marches out through the glowing mouth.
Kit parts: tile-capped wall, square tower, glowing gate arch, stepped keep, square chimney.
- **Tier 1, Clamp Kiln:** one square kiln and chimney behind a low tile-capped wall.
- **Tier 2, Kiln Yard:** curtain wall, four corner towers, glowing gate, tiled muster floor.
- **Tier 3, the Great Kiln:** a stepped keep roofed in turquoise, two chimneys, a mouth that never goes dark.

## 5. The mark on the land: Fired Ground
**In story.** The Kilnborn were made to carry, and woke. The only safe thing they know is a thing that cannot be reshaped, and land is the biggest thing someone could still make into something else. So they fire what they hold, carefully, in squares: no hollows, no vessels. The living keep their homes, with tile roofs they did not choose.

**Three stages.**
1. **Pegged** (newly taken): turquoise-capped posts at corners and crossings, the road edge relaid in cream tile, one clamp kiln smoking on the nearest rise. Fields still green.
2. **Fired** (held a while): fields cut into squares and fired to terracotta and cream tile, green in the joints. Glazed-tile roads, streams between turquoise brick, turquoise cottage roofs, tile-capped walls for hedges.
3. **Glazed** (heartland): one tiled floor that flashes in the Held Sun like glaze, trees in neat rows set into it. Handsome and still.

**When they lose it.** It never fades. The glaze goes matte, the kilns go cold, but the tile stays, and the new owner's mark must grow through the joints: wax pools in the cracks, briar splits the tile. It scars. It does not fight.

**On the 3D board.**
- *Ground:* one tile atlas (terracotta, cream, green joints), masked in by stage.
- *Props:* instanced capped posts, tile-capped wall pieces, one square-kiln mesh, a turquoise material swap on cottage roofs.
- *Border edge:* a row of capped posts and a cream-tile line.
- *Light and particles:* a specular glint on tile, thin chimney smoke.
- *Quality:* low keeps tile ground and roof swap; medium adds posts and walls; high adds kilns, smoke and glint.

**Rule seed: Hardpan.** Ground held three turns is fired. If anyone but the Kilnborn takes it, it pays them 1 less gold for 3 turns. Tooltip: "Fired ground: baked hard. Whoever takes it earns less from it for a while." Design owns the numbers.

## 6. Rivalry and ally
**Sharpest rivalry: the Thornmane Clans.** Briar splits tile, and the Clans attack into woods a fired province no longer has.
**Unlikeliest ally: the Candlewake.** A kiln melts wax, but the Candlewake want a death finished and the Kilnborn want a world finished, and neither needs the maker back.
