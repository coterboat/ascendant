# Veiled Table: Second Dossier

## What changed

Everything mechanical changed. Whispered Terms is gone: the Table now takes a distinct intelligence turn, narrows fog estimates, builds temporary sight, negotiates conditional Free Hold access, and may create explicitly uncertain viewer-only decoys.

## Rulers and commander

**First Host Nella** is a mortal-line organizer chosen because she can hold seven incompatible truths without crowning one. She wants the Open Evening: moving light, open roads and local law. Her game spends power on mandates and uses a mobile second-rank ruler to **Open the Ledger**, trading movement for bounded intel. If killed, an heir arrives in two turns with half power. Sacrifice buys a stronger federation but makes frontline investigation dangerous.

**The Banked Welcome** is an undying civic being re-formed from coals freely carried to Commonroof. It rules because every household has literally entrusted it with warmth, and wants hospitality to become a law of nature. Its game keeps power for front-rank survival while operating on a contested frontier; if broken, it reforms at Commonroof after four turns. Sacrifice shifts strength from this singular anchor into mandates and the network.

The **Pact Herald** is a field witness, courier-master and commander. Its tall smoking standard provides an unmistakable rear-rank silhouette and makes a temporary Listening Post believable on the board. It coordinates ordinary troops rather than secretly assassinating rivals.

## Commonroof

Seven feuding hill towns built Commonroof by joining fortified houses without demolishing their many doors. Its central muster is a table court: household militias gather at folding maps, then carry the tables out as carts. Tier one is a **Joined Court** of cream house-walls, open gates and one smoke tower. Tier two adds repeating roof towers, balconies and a larger council hall. Tier three is **Commonroof Many-Doored**, a full inhabited ring with several gates and a commanding hall. Houses, roof towers, gates, hall and courtyard make the modular kit.

## Mark on the land

The Table makes relationships physical. New provinces show desire paths from doors to road. Held land gains ochre footpath webs, small teal awnings, copper bowls and open cream lintels through hedges. Heartland has every dwelling linked to bridge, refuge and meeting place, with rhythmic smoke curls. On loss, smoke stops and awnings go; paths linger until disuse, often fighting a new mark by channeling travel along old agreements.

Cheap board treatment: ochre path decals, sparse awning/bowl/lintel props, teal accents, and a few smoke particles at the border. Low settings remove smoke, then props, then secondary paths. Rule seed: Listening Posts established in heartland could last one extra turn; design owns tuning.

Sharpest rivalry: **Aurel Crown**, one sovereign truth against negotiated legitimacy. Unlikeliest ally: **Thornmane Clans**, whose face-to-face oaths are blunt but exceptionally reliable intelligence.
