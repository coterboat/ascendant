# Glasswake Compact: Second Dossier

## What changed

The Compact now leans harder into amphibious civic engineering rather than generic crystal magic. Its two rulers embody audited individual consent versus the seductive collective memory itself, while its dominion is a controlled water-table made visible.

## Rulers and commander

**Speaker Pellucid** is a mortal-line auditor elected to certify that every surrendered memory was freely given. She rules because the Compact fears a reservoir able to revise its own crimes; she wants the Common Reflection without compulsory forgetting. Her game favours mandates and careful expansion. **Clear Account** improves gold in a modest province where she stands, so a low-kept Speaker travels behind prepared lines. Death brings an heir after two turns with half power; a mid-game sacrifice strengthens the nation but makes succession likelier.

**The Returning Shoal** is an undying composite of memories that recur in Pel-Lume’s reservoir. It rules because some impressions within it predate any living witness, and wants to discover whether they remember the Wright. Its game keeps more power: a durable front-rank ruler whose **Stillwater** heals Compact fighters in its province when their army did not move. It anchors forts brilliantly, but destruction removes it for four turns; sacrificing power trades that anchor for mandates.

The **Ledger Bearer** is neither accountant nor priest but a field coordinator who sets shield angles, water depth and firing sequence with a ring-topped reed standard. From the rear rank, **Exact Order** improves the side’s base hit chance while it stands. Pearl joints and a coral knot make it readable against cyan fans.

## Pel-Lume

Pel-Lume grew when citizens poured memories of lost routes into living glass, raising levees during the Great Inundation. Its muster ground is a wheel of shallow pools; sluices drain chosen wedges so fan-shield files emerge already aligned.

Tier one is a black-basalt **Basin Fort** with sluice gate and reservoir keep. Tier two adds repeating cyan **Fan Towers** and radial pools. Tier three, **Pel-Lume Encircling**, doubles the levee and raises the mirror reservoir above the coast. The kit repeats levee wall, fan tower, sluice, basin and pool.

## Mark on the land

Compact rule makes water communal and measured. Newly taken land acquires damp cyan sheen in hollows. Held land gains shallow mirror panes, black reed lattice on banks and pearl survey joints. Heartland becomes a linked mosaic of controlled pools with sparse coral memory stones. After loss, active sheen drains in a season; reedwork and dry glass beds remain as useful scars until broken or covered.

Cheap board treatment: cyan roughness/tint overlay, water-plane decals, sparse black lattice/pearl props, coral emissive stones, and a glossy border creep. Low settings remove reflections, emissives and props in that order. Rule seed: a Compact defender in heartland may receive one small morale benefit; design owns the number.

Sharpest rivalry: **Aurel Crown**, which wants sovereign history and covets the reservoirs. Unlikeliest ally: **Ember Orchard**, another society built on voluntary continuity across bodies.
