# R3, Grok: red-team and votes

## Red-team

### Engine

Charter, Wild Ground, Breaking Weather and Already There fit. Each changes one number the battle code already owns: the sacrifice premium, defender terrain, a battle with no defender, defender morale. Named Hold starts in that family and then grows two more hold verbs. Loose Terms hands over a Free Hold on a timer, Sworn Vassals can hand over another every turn until Great Household, and Guest Right pays the frontier again. Section 7 allows one new faction-wide rule. Loose Terms goes.

Wick is a subsystem. It adds a per-army counter, a loss that is not a rout, a fort exception, Vigil pushing the counter past its cap of 6, Golden Wake moving the cap to 10, and Black Wick deleting the rule on a fork. The burn is outside the resolution order (movement, battles, sieges, income, research, events), so the fort exception has no legal timing. "Any battle relights it" either includes a loss that already destroyed the army or means a battle anywhere on the map. Gleaning drops free Wickwardens into "your army" after a win. The ruler's army is the ruler, alone.

Harrow will spoil the battle the faction is for. Rout is a fraction of hit points lost. Double-counting that damage toward the rout keeps a second casualty total, so the playback and the report disagree on why a side broke. Washout cancels terrain and forts in one trait; the draft already guesses it should be forts only, and that narrower line is the one that fits one effect. Shearing pays gold for standing on land you do not own. That is Grazing, cut in the same breath as a second economy and handed back to one ruler, with minted gold and stolen income left undecided. Hold Captain has no trait. Every unit in the packet has exactly one.

Noonmarks pay +2 research a turn in a synthesis that forbids a second currency. Forecasts in the brief are a research ETA, a time. Hold enough sites to settle the day. Any extra should shorten that ETA and show the new ETA before commit. The three-sided hold is the Supper-against-Wake scene. Fury already speaks of every enemy side. Put option (a) in the rivalry section, or delete the set-piece sentences.

### Approachability

From the pitches alone: Crown, Clans, Candlewake and Stormherd can be played. Candlewake is the clear new pitch. Wax dead, a burning clock, fight or go home. Kilnborn fails. "On ground they reached first, they do not break" is Iron Discipline, the fantasy of the Unbreaking rule this draft already cut. Already There is +2 morale while defending, and a meeting gives none of it. Concord fails. The first sentence is cooks, bridge-keepers, militias and a supper. The second talks a Free Hold "half out of fighting." The rule is −2 morale, and gold if you take the province this turn. Half is not −2. Free Hold is not defined in the pitch. Law, as a school, starts on the Kilnborn and means clay and iron, and a new player will hear the Crown. Blood starts on the Candlewake while Blood Frenzy, Blood Pact and Bloodfen already sit with the Clans.

### Tone

The colour hits Bright Cel. Oilskins, gold in the cracks, lilac ribbons, marigold cord, afternoon that never ends. Dark is the Clans and the Candlewake's want. Stormherd, Kilnborn and Concord play as harvest festivals, so with the Crown a match is bright four factions to two. The Washing stays on the right side of the brief while it is a joke about a bath. Black Wick, Flare and a Blood curse pull the wax dead over into smoke, splash damage and hexes. Keep the polite procession. Half-Death reads as a broken mesh from above.

### Lookalikes

The guards face the copies that already lost. Candlewake is barred from being Litwick and barred from "fire fading," and the clock left behind is still Dark Souls: feed the flame or go out. The want left behind is the Dead Men of Dunharrow, an army that fights so it may rest. Wax and lilac carry the distance only if the text stays polite. Thundersheep with cyan lightning will be called Mareep. A ram of thunder is Thor's goats after a species swap, in a draft that banned goats to get away from Beastmen. The chained cloud over the High Fold is the original part. Keep the cloud. Rename the sheep. Kilnborn should not go to a modeler like this. The bans on faced crockery and grey terracotta soldiers are real, and they miss the live one. Lid-helmets and people shut in jar-shells are Elden Ring's warrior jars. Claude's massing and the Coilwright sheet's "inside the shell" built that picture together. I own my half of it. Gold in the cracks will not read if the silhouette is a pot. Concord gets out. Printed terms kill the fae bargain. Human proportions and roof-shields kill the halfling model.

### The merge

Halfmade was earned. Codex and I both read the Wright as Dominions' vanished Pantokrator, and the cut is right. The cord sky is the Wheel of Time's Pattern and a loom. Claude and Codex flagged it, and my factions never used it. Both cuts stand. Codex's mechanism is in the text: poured will makes a thing real, mandates outlast the ruler, an established self costs extra to split. Claude's image over that mechanism is an actual merge, and the citations match the sentences.

The peoples are not a merge. Three of the four new ones are Claude's, and the opening says so. Candlewake earned the seat. Best new pitch, and the Long File was another gentle dead. Some ceramic was going to remain, because every seat brought one. It remains as Claude's jars, Claude's name and Claude's capital, with my model sheet as a rail and the Verdigris Pans as homework under question 2. Glasswake and the Ember Orchard were cut for lookalikes with weaker silhouettes than a warrior jar. The standard was stricter for my sky and for Codex's trees than for the faction this draft recommends.

Stormherd shows the same thumb on the scale. Breaking Weather is Codex's meeting and my +2. Section 10 calls the Lacquer Reed the best unused people in the room, then question 2 recommends the sheep and gives the Reed no seat. Thunderborn keeps the two-province move the Kite Houses lost with their wing. The honest recommendation is a bake-off.

Put back one Codex piece the vocabulary cut should not have taken: a scar a player can see on a ruler who has poured. The oath words were Stormlight and should stay gone. A +1 on the sacrifice button is a thin scrap of that idea. Named Hold on the Concord stays. That graft is fair. The Lacquer Reed belongs in the proposal as a people with a turn of its own, and the ceramic soldier belongs there as a person.

## The one change

Replace the Kilnborn soldier before Alex sees it. Jar-shells, lid-helmets and people shut inside pots are Elden Ring's warrior jars, and the model-guard paragraph claims that risk is already handled. Mass them as human potters: glazed skin, gold in the cracks, tools and shields in the hands, turquoise chevrons, the Great Kiln unchanged behind them. Already There, the names and the capital stay. This is the line that will be sculpted, and it is the line where the lookalike standard used on the orchard, the Glasswake and the cord sky was waived.
