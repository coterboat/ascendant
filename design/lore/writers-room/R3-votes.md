# R3 votes: the room's verdict on the synthesis

All three seats keep the Clans, the Held Sun and Sun magic, and all three vote to change the Concord and the Free Holds' role (each with a different fix), while also flagging Wick, the Kilnborn's "reached first" pitch and the Free Holds' overloaded hooks. They split on the Stormherd (Claude changes the rule, Codex keeps it, Grok keeps the rule but changes Shearing and the sheep name), on Wick (Claude and Grok keep it with fixes, Codex replaces it) and on names (Claude wants Unsettled and Daymarks, while Codex and Grok keep Halfmade and want Quick renamed Making). All three say the merge leaned toward Claude's seat, with Claude calling it "half earned, half home bias," Codex "partly earned and partly home bias," and Grok saying "the peoples are not a merge," and all three say Grok's Lacquer Reed deserved a place.

## 2. Each seat's one change

- **Claude:** "Rewrite Breaking Weather as 'Stormherd regular units have +2 attack in any battle where they are not the defender', and let Floodborn flip it to 'when they defend'." The current trigger is dormant against holds, cancelled by forts and cannot be caused by the Stormherd alone. The cost is that the blind "will they come?" bet moves to the Kilnborn.
- **Codex:** "Replace Candlewake's Wick countdown before Alex sees the proposal." In its place, **Afterglow**: regulars gain +2 morale if their army fought last turn or began in an owned fort, and otherwise take −1 morale outside owned land. This drops per-army countdowns and split-and-merge exploits.
- **Grok:** "Replace the Kilnborn soldier before Alex sees it." Jar-shells and lid-helmets are Elden Ring's warrior jars, so mass them as human potters (glazed skin, gold in the cracks, tools and shields in hand, turquoise chevrons). Already There, the names and the Great Kiln stay.

## 3. The fairness question

- **Claude:** "Half earned, half home bias." The earned half is that all three seats converged on the same four slots, and the merge cut the Wright, Grazing, Kilnwork and the Revel. The bias half: "I lost rules; the others lost looks and worlds," and "'Three of four peoples' counts skin." It also calls the Q2 ballot too narrow.
- **Codex:** "Partly earned and partly home bias." The three silhouettes justify keeping them on art direction, but "it is not defensible as proof that they were the three strongest complete factions." Leaving the Pans and the Reed outside the main six is "the clearest sign of home bias."
- **Grok:** The world was earned, but "the peoples are not a merge." Candlewake "earned the seat," while the ceramic people and the Stormherd show "the same thumb on the scale" and the lookalike standard was "stricter for my sky and for Codex's trees." Its fix: "The honest recommendation is a bake-off."

## 4. Red-team points raised by two or more seats

**Engine fit**
- **Wick is under-specified** (Claude, Codex, Grok). Open cases include splitting and merging armies, ruler armies, the fort exception, Vigil pushing the counter past 6, and a one-unit army farming relights. Grok adds that the burn has no slot in the resolution order.
- **The Free Holds carry too many hooks** (Claude, Codex, Grok). Named Hold, Loose Terms, Sworn Vassals and Wick fuel all draw on one pool, and Q5 (two attackers, one hold) is open. Codex and Grok both single out Loose Terms and automatic annexation.
- **Golden Wake's banked discount is a second currency** (Claude, Codex). Claude proposes a flat 3-gold discount; Codex calls it a "typed coupon ledger."
- **Washout's scope is muddled** (Codex, Grok). It cancels terrain and forts while the text says fort-washing waits for sieges, and Grok would narrow it to forts.
- **Shearing pays gold from land you may not own** (Codex, Grok), with theft versus new gold undecided.

**Approachability**
- **The Concord pitch says talk, the rule says debuff plus bounty** (Claude, Codex, Grok). Grok: "Half is not −2." Hearth's "lower one hold's morale" repeats it (Claude, Codex).
- **The Kilnborn pitch ("reached first") misdescribes a defender-only rule** (Claude, Codex, Grok). Claude renames the rule, Codex wants "defending ground they already hold," and Grok says the pitch is the cut Unbreaking rule.
- **Quick reads as speed** (Codex, Grok), and Law is generic (Codex: Shaping; Grok: Shape). Claude keeps both names.
- **The ceramic slot is the weakest and the Lacquer Reed was excluded** (Claude, Codex, Grok). Codex and Grok both ask for a bake-off.

**Lookalikes and tone**
- **Kilnborn** (Claude, Codex, Grok). Claude cites clay golems and the Earthen, Codex cites Gorons, Black Mages and kintsugi, and Grok cites warrior jars. Claude calls the model guard "the right defence," while Grok says it misses the live risk.
- **Candlewake reads as Dark Souls** (Codex, Grok), pushed further by Black Wick and flame-headed troops. Claude thinks the guards hold.
- **Thundersheep and Stormlings read as Mareep** (Codex, Grok). Claude's own flag is Weatherwife against Weatherwax.
- **The cosmology still echoes Dominions** (Claude, Codex), even with the Wright cut.
- **Tone runs too bright and domestic** (Claude, Grok). Claude: candles, sheep, pots and a dinner party. Grok: four bright factions to two. Codex's related point is that the set is too tidy an elemental quartet.
