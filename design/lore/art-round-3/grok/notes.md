# Notes for Alex

Tool: Grok Imagine in Grok Build. New scenes used `image_gen`. Faces and buildings that had to stay themselves used `image_edit` on the round-2 portraits and fortresses: the Kiln-Mothers, the Unfired, Half-Death's split face on the host sheet, Nella in the war room, and the three melt stages of one stone fort. The image API does not return a model name, so the model is Grok Imagine and nothing finer.

The melt is one fortress and one camera, three times. Left, just taken: candles on the battlements and the first drips. Middle, held a while: wax over the walls. Right, heartland: a fortress-shaped candle. I generated the stone fort, edited each stage from it, and stitched the three with no captions, so the seam is only the edge of the grass. It is meant as the 3D building-swap sheet.

Kiln-Mothers are three elders now, edited from the round-2 portrait: a long nose in front with the paddle, a broad face, a pointed chin, copper staples, turquoise eyes, indigo ground, the shipped portrait frame. The Unfired keeps that round-2 face and is shown full length, house-tall, with a stranger's thumbprint in the chest and fired soldiers no taller than the toes.

Chimewater, the Long Vigil and the Great Kiln are places people live in, not the fortress kit. The wind-section sheet is eight bodies on one baseline: recorder, shielded pipe, shawm, bagpipe, panpipes, folded bass, coiled horn, and the Pale Note with the fork. The Chormeister is in the text as the cornett with the baton, kitbashed from the horn, and is not a ninth figure on that sheet. The leftmost recorder on the sheet also carries a spear; Yssa's held item in the rules is still the three-hole reed.

A goat-horned enemy line was thrown out of the chord picture and replaced with Crown soldiers in plain blue and gold. A skull on the Long Vigil cobbles was painted out. Half-Death on the host sheet is bare-headed, face split white and lilac outline.

Strongest image this round: `candlewake_capital.png`, the Long Vigil as a city. Wax in the streets, lilac in the windows, the living and the dead in the same market, the great candle on the hill under the Held Sun.
