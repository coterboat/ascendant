# The Glasswake Compact

## Pitch

Amphibious artisans grow glass armor by surrendering chosen memories into communal reservoirs. They are exacting defenders: occupy the ground one turn early, set their fan-shields, and dare the enemy to break a people who literally remember together.

## Origin, choice, and the Wright

The first Glasswake lived on tidal shelves where every generation lost homes to the sea. A glazier discovered that a memory freely relinquished could seed living glass: recollection became structure, translucent and stronger for being shared. During the Great Inundation, citizens gave up private routes, faces and songs so Pel-Lume’s levees could grow overnight. They survived, but afterward nobody wholly owned the past. The Compact chose to continue. At adulthood each citizen selects a memory for the reservoir, not as taxation but as proof that survival is not merely personal. Dissenters fear a society able to edit its own shame; auditors therefore record the shape of every gift, never its content.

Compact schools teach that the Wright is a useful hypothesis corrupted by nostalgia. If a maker existed, it left no reliable memory; therefore claims about its intent are inadmissible. Some Returning Shoals privately believe the reservoirs contain impressions older than the world, but the state refuses to call evidence a god.

Their ending is **the Common Reflection**. The Held Sun dissolves into a luminous horizon encircling the world; no place owns the light, and every willing memory can be held without decay. Individual privacy survives, but chosen experience becomes an afterlife and a public defense. The nightmare is a flawless society that has surrendered every inconvenient recollection.

## Play

**Faction rule — Settled Formation:** Compact regular units get +1 morale when their side is the defender. It uses the engine’s defender state and prints in the report. Each turn asks whether to stop on a Noonmark now and receive the bonus next turn, reinforce a prepared fort, or risk a border battle with no defender. The Compact is tough, not passive: a player who never advances gives away the ascension race.

Rivals: Underweather seeks same-turn collisions before the formation settles. The Aurel Crown covets the reservoirs as archives and hates their refusal of sovereign history. The Thornmane say courage remembered by a basin belongs to nobody.

Rulers: **Speaker Pellucid**, mortal-line, second rank; *Clear Account* adds gold in a low- or middle-income province. **The Returning Shoal**, undying, front rank, anchored to Pel-Lume’s reservoir; *Stillwater* heals Compact units in its province if their army did not move.

Roster: **Shardwarden** — *Set Shields:* +2 defense while defender. **Prism Caster** — *Refraction:* +2 attack while protected in second rank. **Tide Strider** — *Countercurrent:* +2 attack against an enemy that arrived this turn. **Ledger Bearer** (commander) — *Exact Order:* allied fighters use 64% base hit chance while it stands.

## Art direction and risks

Army massing is cyan fan-shields around black vertical frames with diagonal prism rods. Pearl joints and coral-orange memory knots ensure light/dark/accent separation. The charcoal banner bears an orange broken circle crossed by a cyan wave. Pel-Lume is a stepped glass basin inside black levees; radial knee-deep pools are the muster ground and drain to expose files. The capital’s mirror reservoir is its beacon. Nonhuman reed frames and pearl joints avoid elves and fishfolk; accounting knots and surrendered memories avoid generic crystal mystics.
