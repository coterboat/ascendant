# The Veiled Table

## Pitch

The Veiled Table is a federation of envoys, listeners, bridge-keepers and household militias that conquers by knowing what everyone needs before anyone knows it is negotiating. It turns Free Holds through prepared terms, then fights as a loose intelligence network of couriers, roof-shields and mobile field tables rather than as another ordered kingdom.

## Origin, choice, and the Wright

Commonroof began when seven feuding hill towns discovered that the same itinerant cooks had carried recipes, gossip and warnings between all of them. Instead of crowning a mediator, the towns gave those travelers legal standing: anyone who had eaten under three roofs could witness a treaty. The custom grew into the Veiled Table. Its people chose interdependence after heroic rulers turned certainty into ruin. They keep many loyalties and treat a meal as intelligence: who sits where, who serves, who is absent.

They neither affirm nor deny the Wright. Their doctrine says that if a maker exists, creation was an opening offer, not a command. The absence of a signature is meaningful: Halfmade belongs to whoever can reach workable terms now. Priests call this evasive; the Table calls it refusing to forge a name.

They want **the Open Evening**: the Held Sun remains low but begins a slow circuit, so no single noon or night becomes absolute. Every place keeps local law, while roads, asylum and parley become facts of nature. Their danger is paternalism: a network that knows every need can quietly decide every outcome.

## Play

**Faction rule — Whispered Terms:** each turn, name one Free Hold next to your land. Its garrison has −2 morale against you that turn; if you take it that turn, gain 2 gold per garrison unit. The forecast prints both changes. A turn asks which hold to soften, whether to commit before a rival creates a three-sided battle, and where to place troops under a commander.

Rivals: the Aurel Crown sees deniable influence as law without responsibility; the Table sees the Crown as one brittle source of truth. The Thornmane respect face-to-face oaths and despise dossiers. The Ember Orchard argues that belonging should outlive negotiation; the Table insists consent must be renewed by the living.

Rulers: **First Host Nella**, mortal-line, second rank; *Guest Right* makes a province taken within two turns earn +2/3/4/5 gold while she is there. **The Banked Welcome**, undying, front rank, reforms in Commonroof’s common oven; *Loose Terms* makes one adjacent Free Hold join every 4/3/2/2 turns.

Roster: **Doorwarden** — *Threshold:* +2 defense while its side is defender. **Road Slinger** — *Waystone:* +2 attack outside land owned at turn start. **Table Beast** — *Fed Well:* +2 defense while its commander stands. **Pact Herald** (commander) — *Known Terms:* enemy morale penalties on its side are 1 smaller.

## Art direction and risks

From above, armies are low loose constellations: cream roof-shields, handcarts and tall smoke standards, never Crown rectangles. Dark teal wool is the dark area; cream plaster the light; copper, warm wood and marigold cord carry identity. The banner is a cream doorway shaped like an eye above a copper bowl. Commonroof is a linked-house fortress riddled with balconies and several gates; its muster ground is a courtyard of map-tables that fold into carts. The design avoids cozy-halfling comedy through varied human proportions, severe shields, coded smoke and political tension; it avoids generic rogues through civic, public intelligence rather than black leather assassins.
