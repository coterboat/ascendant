# Codex art-pass notes

- **Image tool/model:** OpenAI's built-in `image_gen` tool, default model (the tool did not expose a model identifier). Every final image was generated directly with it and saved as PNG.
- **Visual intent:** each faction owns a strategy-camera massing language as well as a palette: Veiled Table roof-shields and smoke standards; Glasswake fan-shields and black frames; Ember Orchard crowns and split seed-pods; Underweather circles, rain rods and vats. The lineups exaggerate those class silhouettes for stylised 3D translation.
- **Naming:** the `hearthbound` slug is retained, but the proposed in-world name is **the Veiled Table**. It preserves hospitality as political technology while making the faction decisively about diplomacy, intelligence and prepared terms.
- **Strongest faction:** **the Ember Orchard**. Its consent-based composite ancestors give the roster emotional depth, its Returned Seed rule expresses that story with existing battle/recruitment levers, and the charcoal/cream/magenta/orange body language is the most immediately ownable visual identity in this seat.
- **Vista prompt:** Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: wide world vista for a 3D fantasy strategy game. Primary request: Halfmade beneath the Held Sun, a bright colorful high-fantasy world visibly unfinished at continental scale. A low golden sun hangs fixed at the horizon. Finished green plains, forests, hills, rivers, tiny fortresses and marching armies give way to Noonmarks where reality stops mid-stroke: a mountain peak ends as glowing construction lines and flat raw color planes; a waterfall pours from an unpainted hole in the sky; a stair descends into earth and emerges higher in the clouds; a white-gold ribbon of making connects these tall sites to the sun. At the far edges, terrain is sculpted but uncolored, then only luminous outlines over deep indigo emptiness. Include subtle distant material hints of glass facets, ember orchards, linked roof towns, and copper storm rings without foreground faction focus. Bright Cel art direction, stylized 3D, flat saturated color, thick clean outlines, one hard shadow and one highlight, dark but not grim, highly readable shapes, panoramic elevated strategy-map vista. Original cosmology. No text, letters, captions, labels, UI, logos, watermark, signature, recognizable IP.
