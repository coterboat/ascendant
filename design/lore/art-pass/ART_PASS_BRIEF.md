# ASCENDANT lore room, round 4: the art pass

You are one seat of a three-lab art team (Claude, Codex, Grok) for ASCENDANT, a simultaneous-turn fantasy strategy game in the spirit of Dominions. The game is going 3D: a 3D world map where armies muster in fortresses and spill out to war, and 3D battles under a clean 2D interface. The lore room has proposed a world. Alex, the owner, wants to **see** the factions before he decides anything, so this round is about art and depth. It is also a competition: the best images and ideas win their slots.

## Read first (in this folder)
- `R3-synthesis.md`: the current proposal (world "Halfmade", six factions, Free Holds, Noonmarks, magic seeds).
- `PACKET.md`: §3 is the style bible's key parts (the game's look); §7 is the engine's constraints.
- Your own seat's pitches: `R2-<seat>.md`, and for Grok also `R1-grok-full.md`.

## Alex's direction (2026-10-05), which overrides the synthesis wherever they differ
- **The Wright is a mystery.** Some peoples believe a maker made the world and walked out; some deny it; some think something else entirely. Those beliefs drive different motivations, ideologies and magic. Give every faction a clear stance on the Wright.
- **"The Making" replaces "Quick"** as the name of the world's one power: something Made the world. No name is final yet.
- **Depth:** for every faction, its backstory, and above all *why* its people chose (or refused) to be what they are.
- **The Hearthbound Concord sits too close to the Aurel Crown**, since Crown vs Clans is already order vs chaos. Alex likes the idea of a **diplomacy and espionage, big-brain faction** with its own way of playing. If that's your faction, rework it in that direction so it reads apart from the Crown.
- **Candlewake:** Alex likes both Wick and Afterglow, and momentum play (Khorne's blood momentum in Total War: Warhammer 3; Lu Bu in Total War: Three Kingdoms). His image: **a shambling mass of the dead who must keep harvesting the essence of the living to keep their flames alight.** It's vampire-adjacent but different enough. If that's your faction, build on it.
- **Two players attacking one Free Hold** fight one three-sided battle, the hold defending against both, and then the attackers turn on each other.
- **The faction ballot is open again** after this pass. A left-out faction can win its way back in on the strength of its art and its story.

## Your factions
- **Claude:** the Candlewake, the Stormherd and the Kilnborn (answer the "warrior jar" lookalike worry with your design), plus your left-out **Revel**.
- **Codex:** the Hearthbound Concord (reworked as above, under a new name if you like), plus your left-out factions: **the Glasswake Compact, the Ember Orchard and the Underweather Assembly**. Champion at least two of them; all three is better.
- **Grok:** your left-out factions. Champion 3 or 4 of **the Verdigris Pans, the Lacquer Reed, the Kite Houses, the Long File and the Factor Houses** (the Lacquer Reed and the Verdigris Pans are the room's favourites).
- The Aurel Crown and the Thornmane Clans are built and have art. Leave them alone, except to say how your factions relate to them.

## Deliverables (all in your seat's output folder)
1. **One dossier per faction**, `dossier_<faction>.md`, 600–1,000 words:
   - its two-sentence pitch;
   - its origin and backstory, and why its people chose or refused what they are;
   - its stance on the Wright;
   - the ending it wants for the Held Sun;
   - how it plays: its one faction rule and what that does to a player's turn, using the engine's terms;
   - its rivals and why;
   - its rulers in brief, and its roster seed (3 units and a commander, one trait each);
   - its art direction: silhouette and massing as read from a strategy camera, palette and materials, banner, fortress and capital, muster ground;
   - lookalike risks, and how this design avoids them;
   - **the exact image prompts you used.**
2. **Three images per faction**, PNG, at the best size your tool gives:
   - `<faction>_key.png`: key art. The people in their homeland, at their fortress or muster ground, under the Held Sun's low golden light. Wide.
   - `<faction>_lineup.png`: a lineup or model sheet. Ruler, commander and the 3 units side by side on a plain ground, readable as silhouettes, as direction for stylised 3D models seen from a strategy camera. Wide.
   - `<faction>_banner.png`: the banner or emblem. Bold, readable small, invented heraldry. Tall.
3. **One world vista per seat**, `vista.png`: Halfmade under the Held Sun. Make the half-made, unfinished quality of the world visible. Wide.
4. **`notes.md`**: which image tool and model you used, anything you'd want Alex to know, and your pick for your seat's strongest faction and why.

## Rules for the images
- **No text, letters, captions, labels, watermarks or signatures anywhere in any image.** They are judged blind, so seat names must never appear.
- The look: bright, colourful high fantasy, "dark but not grim". The game's own style is cel-shaded, with flat saturated colour, thick clean outlines and strong silhouettes (style bible). Lean toward it so the art can steer the 3D look, but stylisation that serves the faction is welcome.
- **Original only:** no thinly veiled elves, dwarves, orcs, undead legions, Warhammer, Dominions, Elden Ring, Dark Souls or any recognisable IP. Each faction must read apart from the other five at a glance (silhouette, palette, materials).
- If a result is weak, re-roll it. Keep only your best image per slot.
