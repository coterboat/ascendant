# The Verdigris Pans

## Pitch

The Verdigris Pans are salt-rakers of ordinary height who wear the white crust of an unfinished plain as armour, and who want the Held Sun scored with lines that can be raked again. On a plains province they already hold, that empty ground is a wall.

## Origin

Where the Making stopped, it stopped flat. The brine ate their tracks, so they raked it into crust and wore the crust. The First Rake is the choice: a youth stands in a raked circle until the salt becomes plates. The Wetfeet refuse and are counted, so the choice stays visible. They refused kilns and charters: a fired plate cannot be raked back, and a read-aloud law is a roof.

## The Wright

Wardens teach that a maker crossed the pans and left, and that the crust is the crossing gone dry. They do not want the maker home. The Wetfeet say brine crusts because brine does. An absent maker cannot overrule a rake.

## The ending

The Fresh Rake draws the Held Sun into a salt disc scored with living lines, open to another season.

## How it plays

**Open Pan.** Defending a plains province gives the side +2 defense. Forest, hills, meetings, and border battles pay nothing. A plains fort still adds its own +2. The forecast shows the bonus, or shows it missing if both armies will arrive together. In a three-sided attack on a Free Hold, the hold defends, so Open Pan is off. Meetings strip Set too.

## Rivals

The Kilnborn want any ground they reached first: on your plain, defense meets their morale, and on a hill the hill is theirs. The Thornmane want the wood. The Kite Houses own the slopes. Crown knights want a meeting, where the crust is off.

## Rulers and roster

**Warden Brack**, mortal line, front, crust shield and verdigris yoke. **Dry Tithe:** +2/3/4/5 gold in his province. Heir in two turns at half power.

**The Kept Crust**, undying, second rank, tied to Dryhold's seal-stone. **Seal:** heal 2/4/7/10 hit points a turn on wounded there. Re-forms in four turns at full power.

Sketches are hp/attack/defense/morale/cost.

- **Crustguard**, heavy infantry, 11/8/10/9/10. **Set:** +2 defense while its side is the defender.
- **Brine Slinger**, second rank, 8/11/5/7/9. **Weight:** +2 damage versus a higher-defense target. No Volley shield.
- **Pan Ram**, monster, 22/10/9/8/22. **Brace:** the front rank has +1 defense while it stands.
- **Pan Marshal**, commander, 24/9/12/12/34. **Hold Fast:** fighters have +1 defense while it stands.

## Art direction

From a strategy camera, a ring of low white discs, the ram a wider wedge, green yokes the strong colour, the marshal's pole the tall piece. Light is salt-white. Dark is charcoal and the seal-stone. The accent is oxidized green on cracked mineral, over human sunburnt faces. The banner is salt-white, a charcoal border, and a verdigris U under a bar. **Dryhold** is a low circular crust-wall, one notch of a gate, a dark seal-stone, and no tower. Muster, the Pan, is the flat inside the ring: companies form circles and leave by the notch.

## Lookalikes

Ordinary height, open sky, opaque salt, a green yoke, and a horned ox keep the read off dwarf-halls, glass, glazed jars, and white knights. The Crown is a spear block. The Pans are a white ring.
