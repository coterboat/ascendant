# The Long File

## Pitch

The Long File are living porters in cream cloaks and soot harness who march as one column and pay themselves for the soldiers who come home. They want the Held Sun carried the last mile and set down. After a win they gain gold for each regular still standing, so the turn is whether to win clean.

## Origin

They carried other nations' kits on unfinished roads, and the old file paid a death-wage until the column was a walking funeral. The Shut Ledger broke that. Three speakers buried the fallen under the road and took the wage from whoever came home. The File will not march beside a flame that spends a corpse. They kept the column because it has no throne. The Set-Downs carry one season and then stop, and that day is a feast. The Closed Tally, the old board, is kept to hold the line and forbidden to pay anyone.

## The Wright

"Wright" is what other nations say so they can drop the load. If a maker exists, the maker is a porter still on shift, and the File will not worship the shift. A younger line says there was no maker. The public creed cares who is still carrying it.

## The ending

The Set-Down puts the sun on the ground so the day can move. The Free Holds love the endless afternoon. The File call that a shift.

## How it plays

**Paid Step.** After a win, gain 2 gold for each regular still standing. Nothing on a loss. Commanders and rulers do not count. The forecast shows a range. A narrow win pays less, so you decline a grind. In a three-sided battle the gold comes only if the File's side wins. Ivo pays only on a turn he did not fight.

## Rivals

The Candlewake want any fight. You want a clean one, which is why the death-wage was buried. The Thornmane want you hurt in the wood. The Crown is a block of spears, and you want them off their fort. A shared hold is three-sided, and the hold defends against both.

## Rulers and roster

**First Counter Ivo**, mortal line, front, copper tally-rod, apricot showing. **Quiet Road:** +3/4/5/6 gold if he did not fight. Heir in two turns at half power.

**The Closed Tally**, undying, second rank, a blank board worn like a door. **Spare the Count:** morale counts 1/2/3/4 higher in battles there. Re-forms in four turns at full power. It moves the break line and does not heal.

Sketches are hp/attack/defense/morale/cost.

- **File Guard**, infantry, 11/8/8/9/10. **Close File:** +2 defense while another File Guard stands.
- **Marker**, second rank, 8/11/5/7/9. **Mark:** a target it has hit has −2 defense against your other fighters. No Volley shield.
- **Yoke Beast**, monster, 20/11/7/8/20. **Drag:** +2 attack while a File Guard stands. Hornless, under a tall copper frame.
- **File Speaker**, commander, 22/8/11/12/33. **Keep the Count:** regulars have +2 hit points this battle while it stands.

## Art direction

From above, a cream-and-soot line, longer than wide, the copper frame the tall shape, apricot flashing as a cloak turns. Light is cream. Dark is soot harness. The banner is cream, a soot border, and a shallow apricot bar with a solid copper disc, where the Pans' mark is a deep green U. **Longshed** is a roofless copper shed, one lane, no flame. Muster is that lane, single file out the mouth.

## Lookalikes

Living faces, cream, and apricot, with no flame and no skull, keep them off the Candlewake. Pride and copper keep them off a ragged column. Solo cloaks keep them off the Concord's tables and pairs. An open shed keeps them off a dwarf-hall. The Crown is a block. The File is a line.
