# Notes for Alex

## Tool

Every image was made with Grok Imagine in Grok Build: `image_gen` for new pictures and `image_edit` for corrections and for scenes that had to keep a lineup's people. The rate-limit notice named the model `grok-imagine-image-quality`. The tool returned JPEG. I converted each shipped file to PNG with `sips`. Wide images are 1280×720. Banners are 832×1248. No prompt asked for letters, and none of the shipped images carry captions, watermarks, or seat names.

## What I championed

Four factions: the Lacquer Reed, the Verdigris Pans, the Kite Houses, and the Long File.

I left the Factor Houses out. Their rule, Named Hold, and their paired-coat body were given to the Hearthbound Concord in the synthesis. You have asked for that seat to be reworked into a diplomacy and espionage faction that sits apart from the Crown, and that rework belongs to the seat that owns the Concord. A second hold-talker from me would have stood on their feet. The four here each own a different last click.

The Wright is folklore, and the four stances disagree on purpose.

- The Pans say a maker crossed the salt and left, and that the leaving is a gift. Their Wetfeet deny any maker at all. Both stay in the people.
- The Reed's orthodox song says there is no Wright, because the marsh was already an instrument. A minority verse says the maker thinned away into a note. Their god is offended to be called the Wright.
- The Kite Houses say the Wright is still above, holding the sun and forgetting the string. They climb to cut the string, not to meet the hand. One house thinks the holder is a person. One house thinks "Wright" is a face painted on a snag.
- The Long File say "Wright" is a compliment other nations pay so they can put the load down. If there is a maker, the maker is a porter still on shift, and they will not worship the shift.

The world's one power is the Making, in the ruler, in the people, and in the Noonmarks. I did not bring back the cord sky.

## Rules, in engine words

Design owns every number. Each faction has one rule.

- **Open Pan** (Pans). +2 defense when they defend a plains province. Meetings, border battles, forest, and hills do not pay. This is not the Kilnborn's Already There, which is morale on any defense. In a three-sided attack on a Free Hold the hold is the defender, so Open Pan is off.
- **Low Note** (Reed). In a forest they already hold, their second-rank fighters deal +2 damage on a hit. Their singers stand in the second rank and do not gain Volley's shield, so the chord can be killed. A three-sided forest hold turns the rule off. If the Clans attack a wood the Reed already hold, Wild Ground and Low Note can both be true, and the report should name both.
- **Higher Air** (Kite Houses). Mounted fighters have +2 attack in hills, whether they attack, defend, or meet. No extra movement, and forts still work. This is not Breaking Weather, which pays every regular in any meeting. On a hill hold fought three-sided, Higher Air still pays, because it never asks who the defender is.
- **Paid Step** (Long File). After a win, 2 gold for each regular unit still standing. Nothing on a loss. Commanders and rulers do not count. This is the inverse of a death-wage. Golden Wake can bank the fallen. The File broke with that idea, and the break is the faction. The 2 can come down to 1 if the purse bends the map.

Second-rank units in these rosters are placed like Volley units and do not receive Volley's shielding. That is deliberate. The Reed's damage, the Pans' anti-wall slings, and the File's marks all live in bodies an enemy can reach.

## The art, briefly

The Reed took the most passes. Early sheets put human heads under bell-hats. The shipped people are the pipe itself, face inside the bone-white cone, violet resin at the lip. The Kite sheets first drew soldiers holding flags, then clawed feet. The shipped mounts are indigo diamonds on plain reed poles, with a wingless stake soldier beside them and a riderless sail as the god. The Pans' shield mark was corrected from a stem that read as a letter into a wide U under a bar, and the armour was roughened into cracked salt. The File's banner buckle was corrected so it is a solid disc.

Palettes, for the glance test: salt-white, charcoal, verdigris; lacquer teal, bone-white, violet; near-black indigo, straw-gold, vermilion ribbon; cream, soot, apricot, copper. None of these is the Crown's blue and gold or the Clans' tawny, bone, moss, and crimson.

## Vista prompt

A wide bright cel-shaded landscape of a world left half finished, under a huge low golden sun. The near left is fully painted: a cracked white salt plain with a low ring wall, a dark marsh crowded with teal pipes on barges, and a hill of indigo silk roofs trailing vermilion ribbons. Exactly at the middle of the picture the paint stops. The right half is raw and unfinished: flat unpainted fields of tan and violet, hills drawn only as glowing gold outlines, a mountain cut off in mid-air as a wire of light, and a tower-sized golden nail that is solid at the top and only a contour below. Thick black outlines, flat saturated colour, one hard shadow, colourful and not grim. No people in the foreground and no writing.

## Strongest faction

The Lacquer Reed. The body is a new silhouette, a comb of pipes with the face inside the bell, and it does not borrow a jar, a shroud, a roof, or a beast. The choice to be hollowed is a real choice, because the Solid and the Half-poured stay in the nation and are honoured for refusing. Low Note gives the player a last click the other five do not have: sit in the wet wood and keep two singers alive, or step out and lose the song. The Thornmane want the opposite click in the same province, which is a rivalry the report can say in two lines.
