# The Lacquer Reed

## Pitch

The Lacquer Reed are hollow marsh people, a lacquered pipe for a body and a living face inside a bone-white bell, who want the Held Sun to settle on one tone. In a forest they already hold, the singers behind the wall hit harder, so the turn is whether the chord is still standing.

## Origin

Fisher-folk heard the marsh reeds in the golden wind, a chord missing a voice. The Hollowing is the choice: drink reed-liquor and become a pipe with a bell for a head, and you may stop. Half-poured stop halfway; the Solid refuse, pole the boats, and both are counted. They refused an undying body, since lacquer cracks and a violet patch is a brag. A front with no singer is the fear.

## The Wright

The orthodox song says there is no Wright: the marsh was already an instrument. A minority verse says the maker thinned into a note. The Pale Note bristles at the name Wright.

## The ending

The Resolved Chord settles the Held Sun on one tone, and the day ends on a rest. The Crown hears a tone where it wanted noon.

## How it plays

**Low Note.** Defending a forest they hold, second-rank fighters deal +2 damage on a hit. Meetings, borders, plains, and hills pay nothing. Swamp will count as forest. No Volley shield, so the forecast is the singers still standing, and you skip Fangthrowers if they will die. A three-sided forest hold turns it off. A Clan attack on your wood can pay Wild Ground as well.

## Rivals

The Thornmane want you out of the trees. You want them in. Skirls soften the Crown's next swing. The Stormherd want an open meeting, and on a Kilnborn hill you are a guest.

## Rulers and roster

**Yssa of the Three**, mortal line, second rank. **Quiet Audience:** +2/3/4/5 gold if no enemy is in or adjacent. Heir in two turns at half power.

**The Pale Note**, god, second rank. **Fearful Pitch:** enemies here start at −1 morale, or −2 at kept power of 6 or more. Returns next turn at −3.

Sketches are hp/attack/defense/morale/cost.

- **Reedward**, infantry, 9/8/8/8/9. **Dampen:** 1 less damage from each hit, minimum 1.
- **Hole-singer**, second rank, 7/10/6/8/10. **Harmony:** +1 attack to the side while two Harmony fighters stand.
- **Skirl**, infantry, 7/10/5/7/8. **Flat Note:** a fighter it hits has −2 attack on its next attack.
- **Chormeister**, commander, 16/9/8/11/35. **True Pitch:** hit chance on its side cannot fall below 50%.

## Art direction

From above they are a comb of vertical pipes, bone-white bells, violet at the lip. Light is the flare. Dark is the mouth-hole. The body is glossy teal lacquer with reed-gold ribs. The banner is teal, a violet border, one deep notch, and a white reed stem with three finger-holes. **Chimewater** is a fan of pipes on barges around a dark water court. Muster is the Sounding Bowl, a drained basin the comb files through.

## Lookalikes

Faces inside bells keep them off elves and the grave. The pipe is the person, so they are not organ-guns or Kilnborn jars. Concord teal is wool. This teal is lacquer.
